# First 10 Minutes Playtest

## Scope
- Three human players (P1, P2, P3), each completing a separate 10-minute session from a fresh start.
- Preparation only: the sessions have not been run. No installation or network access is required by this plan.
- Goal: assess first impressions, onboarding clarity, controls, navigation, and understanding of the first objective.

## Before each session
- [ ] Use the same available game build, starting state, and setup; record build and setup in RESULTS.csv.
- [ ] Assign a player ID (P1, P2, or P3); avoid recording personal information.
- [ ] Explain: “Play as you normally would. Say what you are trying to do and what feels unclear. You may stop at any time.”
- [ ] Have a timer and an observer ready. Do not explain controls or objectives in advance.

## Timed session (10 minutes per player)
- [ ] 00:00–02:00: Start the timer when control is given to the player. Observe the first action, initial expectations, and understanding of controls.
- [ ] 02:00–05:00: Observe whether the player identifies an objective and navigates toward it; note confusion and missed feedback.
- [ ] 05:00–08:00: Observe attempts at the first meaningful task, mistakes, recovery, and any blockers.
- [ ] 08:00–10:00: Observe independent progress and whether the player knows what to do next.
- [ ] Throughout: record timestamped observations and brief quotes. Avoid hints; record any help given and why. If the player stops early, record elapsed time and reason.
- [ ] At 10:00, end gameplay; record progress without extending the session to force completion.

## After each session (outside the 10-minute timer)
- [ ] Ask: “What was your goal?”, “What was most confusing?”, and “Would you continue playing? Why?”
- [ ] Ask for ease of understanding (1 = very difficult, 5 = very easy).
- [ ] Enter one CSV row per observation or feedback item; repeat session metadata as needed. Use elapsed MM:SS for observations and leave unavailable fields blank.
- [ ] Classify issues as blocker (prevents progress), major (substantial difficulty), or minor (small friction); use none for positive/neutral observations.

## After all three players
- [ ] Compare repeated problems across P1, P2, and P3; distinguish observations from interpretations.
- [ ] Prioritize blockers and issues seen in multiple sessions. Do not generalize beyond these three players.
